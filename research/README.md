# Research

Raw research lives here. Rules are distilled from it into the main folders; this folder may be messy, the rules may not.

## Conventions

- One folder per round: `research/NN-topic/` (e.g. `01-taste-calibration/`).
- Each round records, in files named for what they hold (`PLAN.md`, `sources.md`, measurement files, `results*.md`):
  - the question the round tries to answer
  - sources, with links and the date they were read
  - findings, each marked **verified** (seen in a source or on screen) or **inferred** (our reading, not confirmed)
  - the board that was shown and every vote
- `hig-digest/` is a standing digest of Apple's Human Interface Guidelines with web translations, used by every round.
- Screenshots go in `research/_private/NN-topic/`, which git ignores. Never commit images of third-party products.
- Decisions that come out of a round are recorded in `../decisions.md`, and taste findings in `../preferences.md`.

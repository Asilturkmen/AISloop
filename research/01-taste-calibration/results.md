## Single screens (28 like · 9 dislike · 4 unsure)

**Apple native (iPhone/iPad/Mac)**
- Like: Calendar new-event sheet (dark), Mail inbox, Music library, open note, Reminders home (colour tiles), Weather, iPad Music sidebar (dark), iPad Notes three columns, iPad Settings, Mac Calendar week, Mac Mail glass toolbar close-up, Pages format inspector, Photos library grid, Siri app sidebar.
- Dislike: Health summary (gradient header, cards), Notes format sheet, Control Center (glass tiles), Mail settings window (icon tabs), Save dialog with column browser (dark).
- Unsure: Calendar event popover (tall glass popover).

**Apple web apps**
- Like: App Store Today, App Store app page (dark), developer docs, Maps on phone (bottom sheet).
- Dislike: Apple Music album page (artwork-tinted page background).

**Independent products**
- Like: Bear, Fantastical, iA Writer focus mode, Linear (dark), Mimestream, Raycast (dark), Things.
- Dislike: Craft (pastel-coloured cards), Framework7 iOS mail list ("the lines run into each other, it confuses me"), Puppertino macOS windows (imitation).

**Counter-examples (labelled only after voting)**
- Aceternity feature cards: like → **retracted** in follow-up ("looking again, this really is bad").
- Aceternity glass hero over illustration: like; follow-up: "an impressive entrance; AI slop, but the atmosphere is beautiful".
- Framer AI templates page: like; follow-up: "slop, but maybe the imagery makes it look good".
- Aceternity sparkles: unsure; follow-up: "I don't know why I like it, but it looks good".
- v0 AI template gallery: unsure; follow-up: "the image looks good; maybe it's specific to this image".
- macOS-in-the-browser desktop: unsure ("didn't understand what this was asking" — our caption was unclear; excluded from analysis).

## Method notes for next rounds
- Pairs must compare like with like (same device class, same screen type). p01 failed this.
- Don't ask taste questions about choices that follow from page type (p05). Those are rules for AISloop to decide.
- Keep one subject per image; p09-style marketing pages and app pages should be separate genres, and the Framer item mixed a clean page with sloppy thumbnails.
